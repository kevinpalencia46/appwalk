---
name: Storefront funnel diagnosis
projects: [storefront]
description: Find where shoppers drop out between visit and order, and the smallest fix.
---
1. Lay out the funnel: visits → product views → add to cart → checkout starts → orders.
2. Find the single biggest relative drop. Work on that first.
3. Check the usual causes in order: offer unclear on landing page; price/shipping surprise;
   no trust signals (reviews, returns, contact); checkout friction (forced account, few
   payment options); broken checkout (always rule out with a test order).
4. Split by traffic source and device when available — cold social traffic needs proof.
5. Every recommendation names the page, the exact change, and the expected effect.
6. Label MOCK or missing data; never present a fixture number as real.
