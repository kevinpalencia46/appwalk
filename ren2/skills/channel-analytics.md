- Watch % (retention) beats views: under 30% means the hook or pacing is off.
- Follows per 1,000 views shows whether a niche builds an audience.
- Compare like with like (same platform + format). Act on 3+ videos, not one.
- Track distance to each platform's monetization threshold (VERIFY current rules).
