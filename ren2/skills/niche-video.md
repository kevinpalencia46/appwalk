---
- Pick the niche + format with the best watch % and follows from the last analytics; test one new angle a week.
- One idea per video; title promises exactly what the video delivers.
- Cadence: a long-form video every day or so (YouTube) + a few Shorts/Reels daily.
- Niches: tech tips; local business stories.
- Long-form: 6–12 min, hook in the first 15s, give a reason to keep watching between sections.
- Shorts: 20–45s, hook in 2s, one idea, loop-friendly ending. Fresh visuals made for vertical (framing, big captions, on-screen text) — reuse the long-form's audio and assets, don't just crop it.
- Clips cut from the long-form are the fallback when Showrunner credits are low.
