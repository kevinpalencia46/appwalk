name: Web design (general)
projects: [builder, websites]
description: What every Worklane site must get right. Grows from our review decisions.
---
- Above the fold: what the business does, where, and one clear call to action (call / book / quote).
- Mobile first: tap-to-call, big buttons, readable at arm's length.
- Real details only: hours, service area, phone, address. No fake reviews, stats or logos.
- 3–4 sections max on a demo: services, why us, how it works, contact.
- Fast and plain: system fonts, few images, high contrast.
